Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our African clients

What shipping methods do you offer to Africa?

We offer sea freight (most economical for bulk orders) to all major African ports including Lagos, Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, Durban, and Accra. Air freight is available for urgent/smaller orders. Typical sea freight transit time is 25-40 days depending on destination.

Are your parts genuine or aftermarket?

We supply both genuine OEM parts and high-quality aftermarket alternatives. All aftermarket parts meet or exceed OEM specifications. We clearly indicate the grade of each product so you can make informed decisions based on your budget and requirements.

What is your minimum order quantity?

We accommodate orders of all sizes. For smaller orders, we can consolidate via LCL (Less than Container Load) shipping. For bulk orders, we offer full container load (FCL) with the best per-unit pricing. Contact us with your requirements for a tailored quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Telegraphic Transfer (TT), Letter of Credit (LC) for large orders, and other secure payment methods. Payment terms can be discussed based on order volume and relationship history.

Do you offer warranties on your parts?

Yes, all our parts come with a warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty terms vary by product category and will be clearly stated in your quotation and invoice.
