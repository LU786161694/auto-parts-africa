Our Story

Founded with a clear mission, AutoParts Africa was established to solve a critical problem facing the African automotive market: access to reliable, affordable Japanese auto parts.

With over 10 years of experience in the automotive parts industry, our founders recognized that African businesses and consumers were paying premium prices for substandard parts through inefficient supply chains. We set out to change that.

Today, we operate from strategic sourcing hubs in Guangzhou, China and Dubai, UAE, leveraging established trade routes to deliver quality Japanese auto parts to customers in 15+ African countries.

Why We Focus on Africa

Africa is one of the world's fastest-growing automotive markets

Japanese vehicles dominate African roads. Toyota, Honda, and Nissan are the most trusted and widely driven brands across the continent 鈥?from the Toyota Hilux in South African mines to the Honda motorcycles of West African cities.

However, the supply chain for genuine parts has long been fragmented and expensive. AutoParts Africa exists to streamline this 鈥?connecting African businesses directly to the best sources of Japanese auto parts at competitive wholesale prices.
